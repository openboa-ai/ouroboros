# Asset usage

Use the primary-light identity on Quiet Off-white or transparency and primary-dark on Blue Carbon.
The brand-field treatment uses Quiet Off-white on Terracotta. Preserve the clear space already
built into PNG exports. Minimum digital widths are 24 px for the symbol, 144 px for horizontal
lockups (160 px preferred), 120 px for stacked lockups, and 96 px for the wordmark. The native
16 px symbol is a favicon exception.

Do not type the wordmark, alter its case, recolor individual scales, close the symbol seams,
stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or place the identity on insufficient-contrast backgrounds.
Use SVG/PDF masters for print and high-resolution composition; use the named platform exports
without adding another container or mask.
