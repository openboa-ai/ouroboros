# Sovereign Designation

This document resolves the human sovereign required by the Core Doctrine for repository-level
governance. It records identity; it does not delegate operational authority or enlarge the powers
reserved by the doctrine.

## Current Designation

For repository governance, the human sovereign is represented by the GitHub identity
[`@openboa`](https://github.com/openboa).

Only an explicit authorization attributable to that identity may establish or amend the terminal
objective or Core Doctrine, determine capital and its upper bound, halt, revoke, or retire the
system, approve a net expansion of authority, or change this designation.

An ordinary commit, review, merge, maintainer action, or team decision does not by itself
constitute sovereign authorization. The authorization must identify the sovereign action and its
scope in a durable pull-request record.

## Succession and Uncertainty

A change of designation requires explicit consent attributable to the current designation. The
same pull request must identify the successor representation and when the succession becomes
effective. A proposed successor may not authorize or certify its own succession.

If the current designation, the required consent, or the scope of an authorization cannot be
resolved unambiguously, no sovereign-only action is authorized. Identity recovery or compromise
is handled outside agent authority and may not be inferred by an agent, maintainer, or successor.

The GitHub identity is the durable repository representation of the human sovereign. It is not a
transfer of human sovereignty to an account, platform, team, or automated system.
